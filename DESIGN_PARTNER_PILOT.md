# MYLA design-partner pilot — small builders (Zimbabwe)

Goal: prove the first-job experience with real small builders **before** any
paid marketing. We want 2–3 builders using MYLA for live jobs, not demos.

## Who to recruit
- 1-person builders to crews of ~10 — residential extensions, fit-outs, small
  civils. Zimbabwe-based, price jobs in USD, currently running on
  WhatsApp + paper + a notebook (or Excel).
- Mix: at least one "phones only" builder, one with a laptop on site.
- Recruit through your own network, building suppliers, or the hardware-shop
  grapevine. Warm intros only — no cold ads yet.

## The offer (keep it simple)
- Free Starter access for 90 days (comped in Billing — set plan STARTER,
  comped = true). Then USD 6/month if they want to stay.
- Direct WhatsApp line to you (founder), not a support queue.
- Setup with you on the first job: you sit in on the 30-minute first-job flow.
- In exchange: honest feedback, weekly 20-minute check-in for 6 weeks, and
  permission to anonymise their workflow story into a case study.

## How to run it
1. Create their workspace together. Choose the **smallest team size** so they
   get the new micro experience: just clients, quotes, jobs, contracts,
   invoices, payments, documents and reports.
2. Walk the "Your first job" checklist on a **real** job (a quote they are
   pricing now — not a made-up example). Time it. Stopwatch from first client
   added to first invoice recorded in MYLA.
3. Leave them alone for a week. Then check in: what did they actually use?
   What did they try to do that blocked them?
4. Week 2–6: one short call per week. Watch one screen-share where they do
   their real billing run.

## Feedback script (ask these, listen more than you talk)
1. "Show me how you'd quote this job for a client." (watch, don't help)
2. "What did you expect to happen when you tapped that?"
3. "What's missing that you need every week?"
4. "What did you stop doing on paper/WhatsApp because MYLA has it?"
5. "Would you pay USD 6/month for this after the pilot? Why / why not?"
6. "Who else do you know who runs their jobs like you?"

## Success criteria (before any "for everyone" messaging)
- At least 2 of 3 builders issue a real invoice from MYLA within 30 minutes
  of first login.
- At least 2 of 3 record a real client payment allocated to an invoice
  within the first 2 weeks.
- No pilot workspace requires a data fix by hand (no support SQL).
- At least 2 of 3 say they would pay USD 6/month (or actually convert).
- Checklist feedback collected in writing (see script answers).

## What NOT to do
- Don't enable every section "so they see the full product" — that defeats the
  purpose. Sections are added only when they ask for something missing.
- Don't promise features that aren't shipped (retention, payroll, stock,
  client portal are not in the micro experience).
- Don't market broadly until multi-role and backup-restore proofs are done.

## Tracking
For each pilot: name, company type, team size, start date, time-to-first
invoice, payment allocation done (y/n), sections added and why, weekly notes,
payment intent, referrals. Keep in a private sheet — not in the repo.
